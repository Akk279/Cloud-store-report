Project Objective

Designed an interactive Power BI retail analytics dashboard to analyze sales performance, product trends, outlet profitability, and customer preferences using Blinkit sales data. The goal was to convert raw transactional data into decision-support insights through KPI monitoring and visual analytics.

Problem Statement

Retail stakeholders need visibility into:

Revenue-driving product categories
Outlet-level sales performance
Customer rating and product preference trends
Inventory and visibility factors affecting sales

This dashboard was built to answer those business questions through interactive reporting.

Key Insights Generated
Medium-sized outlets generated the highest sales contribution.
Fruits and snack foods emerged as top revenue categories.
Higher product visibility showed positive correlation with sales.
Outlet type significantly influenced profitability.
Customer ratings showed measurable influence on product sales.
